[39] (major) D6 layer 2 — boot disarmed / RP2350-E9
  QUOTE: The Pico **boots disarmed**: STBY/IN/PWM driven LOW first; consider a ≤ 8.2 kΩ pull-down on STBY for RP2350-E9.

[41] (major) D5 wall_ahead_m derivation
  QUOTE: **`wall_ahead_m`** is the distance along +x to the nearest wall plane implied by a detected tag's pose (AprilTag gives the tag orientation).
  PROBLEM: The validator's only collision gate depends on the single-tag orientation estimate. The project's own research names that estimate the unreliable part of a tag pose (planar pose ambiguity, worst at shallow or frontal views) and recommends using range and bearing instead of the rotation. The ray–plane intersection amplifies orientation error by the tag's lateral offset. For a wall truly 0.55 m ahead, a tag at bearing 30° with a +20°/+30° normal error gives wall_ahead_m = 0.67/0.73 m, which lets FORWARD_25 through where the validator should refuse (< 0.60). At bearing 45° the same errors give 0.75/0.87 m. A normal error that makes the plane near-parallel to +x gives null. The ray also has no width, so a corner strike on an oblique wall is not modelled.
  EVIDENCE: Computed: true 0.55 m, bearing 30°, normal error +20/+30° → 0.67/0.73 m; bearing 45° → 0.75/0.87 m. topic2-fiducial-slam-baseline.md line 49: 'Use tag translation (range, bearing) rather than inverting the full tag rotation, because a rotation flip gets amplified by the lever arm'; line 24: 'single-marker planar pose estimation is fundamentally ambiguous'; line 60: apriltag_ros publishes no pose-ambiguity error.
  FIX: Compute wall_ahead_m conservatively. Take the minimum over both IPPE/ambiguity solutions and over a ±20° (DV) normal uncertainty, clip it to range·cos(bearing) for tags within ±45°, and subtract half the chassis width times |tan(wall angle)|. In W2, measure the tag-normal error against a known wall angle before relying on the plane.
