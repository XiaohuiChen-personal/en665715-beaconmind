regression check against the round-2 findings, and a literal hand-simulation / Monte-Carlo run of

[1] (major) D7 step 3 (association table) / step 4 drift recovery / D8 map `state` enum (v2 cluster 1: drift lock-out, `suspect` with no exit)
  QUOTE: `rejected`, `conflicts` + 1 on the nearest (≥ 2 → `suspect`)
  PROBLEM: Cluster 1 is only half fixed. (a) `suspect` is a map `state` that replaces `confirmed`/`tentative`, and no rule ever changes it back. Drift recovery needs 'single map entry is confirmed'. fix_pose refuses suspect beacons. Step 5 has no branch for a matched suspect beacon. So a suspect beacon stays suspect for the rest of the episode. (b) Step 3 increments `conflicts` before step 4's drift recovery and before the pose source is known. Increments made against a prior that step 4 then rejects (`prior_kept`), or that drift recovery replaces, are never undone. (c) Drift recovery needs ≥ 3 out-of-gate detections in one 60° view. Views with 1–2 tags are the common case: in a 2000-pose sample, 1157 of 2000 poses had ≤ 2 tags visible and only 796 had ≥ 3. So during a scan, beacons are seen twice out of gate and turn suspect before a 3-tag view relocalises.   FIX: Make suspect a flag (`suspect: true`) that sits alongside `state` rather than replacing it. Clear it, with `conflicts` = 0, on any in-gate match, and state that rule in D7 step 3. Apply `conflicts` increments in step 5 and only when `pose_source` ∈ {beacon_fix, relocalised, position_fix}; under `prior_kept`/`odometry`, or after a relocalisation, do not increment.
