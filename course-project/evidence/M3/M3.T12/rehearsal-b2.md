## What was simulated

- **Room:** the surveyed polygon, the driving area (desk-front line), the desk as an occluder, tags with their own
  normals, everything moved into the map frame of the start pose.
- **Camera ("camera" preset):** `beaconmind.camera_model` on `configs/camera.json` (distortion, mount angles). A tag is
  in view when the four corners of its black square are in the picture. That gives the true view (22.4° left, 23.9°
  right of an axis that points 2.5° left) and the near limit: a 160 mm tag centred 0.23 m up is whole only from
  0.74 m (axle midpoint, straight ahead; 0.70 m at 10° left, 0.80 m at 10° right, 1.09 m at 15° right); a 100 mm tag
  centred 0.1825 m up from 0.53 m. The simulator's test agrees with `camera_model.min_full_view_range` to 1 cm (tested).
- **Detector noise:** the figures of `evidence/M1/M1.T8/sim-estimate.md`: one range scale error per episode,
  N(0, 1.1 %), one range offset per episode, N(0, 7 mm), per-detection range sd 1 mm + 0.35 mm·r² (× 1.6 for 100 mm
  tags), bearing sd 0.3°. Placeholders kept: detection probability 0.95, incidence limit 60°.
- **"study" preset:** kinsim_lite's placeholder numbers (60° view, 0.25 m minimum range, range sd 0.01 + 0.03 r,
  bearing sd 1°) with only the geometry changed (Table 3).
- **Odometry:** `study` (kinsim_lite's: 2 % distance noise, 0.3° turn noise, no bias); `drift2` (the main study's
  held-out condition: distance and turn angle over-read by 2 %); `carpet` (a forward move turns the car 15°/m to the
  left, turns reach 98 %; from the Module 9 notes in CONTEXT.md 2026-11-02; the gyro sees it, so the truth path bends
  while odometry reports the bend). The real car's odometry is not measured yet (work list 2.8).
- **Range limit:** the simulator's `max_range_m`: a tag beyond it is not in the observation. The frozen
  `estimate_step` has no range test (`reject_range_above_m` in `configs/dv.json` is read by no code; the study's
  detector stopped at 1.5 m), so no DV override is needed for 2.5 or 3.0 m. On the car the same cut belongs where the
  observation is built. **The LLM's prompt v3.3 says "rejected if … range_m > 2.0" in words:** with a limit above
  2.0 m an LLM arm that follows the prompt rejects what B2 uses, unless that one number is changed for the real-room
  runs.
- **Options:** A = the printed layout (12 tags, 160 mm, centre 0.23 m). B = the same places, 100 mm tags centred
  0.18 m (125 mm sheet on the baseboard). C16 / C20 = A plus 4 / 8 more 160 mm tags. D16 / D20 = the same with every
  tag 100 mm. The extra places come from `layout-search` (greedy, most poses with a possible two-tag fix first).

## The coded procedure in a 3.8 × 5.2 m room: what was found and what was changed

`estimate_step` and `decide_step` are used as frozen (imported). Three things in them assume the 2.25 m arena:

1. **A branch the frozen code does not have ("E5 leg with no forward move and no E4").** E5 picks a sector; if the car
   already faces it and no forward move is allowed, the written procedure picks the same sector again, and
   `kinsim_lite` raises. In the square, a sector towards a wall holds a mapped tag and is blocked beforehand. In the
   room, edges have no tag in view (desk-front line, far walls, the near limit). **With the printed layout at 2.0 m,
   B2 as frozen stops there in 15 of 150 episodes (study odometry) and 16 of 150 (carpet).** `kinsim_room.make_b2_policy()`
   defines the branch the way rule 7b treats a bump: `blocked_heading_deg` = the heading, decide again; a completed
   forward move clears it (rule 7's own reset). With it, 150 of 150 finish; every step before the branch is identical
   to the frozen procedure (tested), and on the study square it changes nothing (tested). This is the only change to
   B2's behaviour, and the real B2 driver needs it. With 20 tags at 3.0 m the branch was not reached (Table 5).
2. **E1 stops after two scans without a new tag.** In the room that is after about 50 steps with 4–7 of 12 tags
   mapped (Table 1a, option A). Waiting for four scans (an option of `make_b2_policy`, not used) makes episodes about
   twice as long (median 90 steps, 26 of 150 reach the budget) and maps 4.5 instead of 4.2 tags: not worth it. E1 is
   left as written, which is also what the LLM's prompt says.
3. **Other unimplemented branches** (relocalisation, drift recovery, ambiguous association) end an episode ("B2
   stopped"). They are rare without drift (0 of 150 in every study and carpet row) and appear with 2 % drift, more
   tags and longer range: up to 14 of 150 (D20, 3.0 m). The records up to the stop are kept.

**Two-tag ("full") fixes need the DV noise model changed, not only more tags.** `fix_pose` accepts a full fix when
its own error estimate passes sigma_xy ≤ 0.15 m and sigma_theta ≤ 8°, computed with the DV noise model of
`configs/dv.json` (range 0.05 + 0.05 r m, bearing 2°: written before any camera existed). For two to four tags on one
wall seen square-on inside a 46° picture that test passes in **0.0 %** of random cases; with the simulated detector's
figures (`kinsim_room.MEASURED_SIGMA_DV`: 0.01 + 0.012 r m, 0.5°) in 34 %, 65 % and 85 % (`fix-conditioning.txt`).
Over the driving area at 2.5 m, the printed layout shows two tags at once from 5.5 % of poses, and a full fix is
possible from 0.0 % (frozen sigmas) or 5.2 % (measured sigmas); eight more tags give 23.1 % and 19.9 %
(`layout-search.txt`). So Table 2 repeats Table 1 with those three sigma values replaced through
`kinsim_room.dv_override` (a rehearsal-only switch on the cached values; no file is edited). For real runs this means
a real-run design-value file with the pilot's measured sigmas, which is Xiaohui's decision.

What the tables say:

- **With the frozen DV sigmas, full fixes stay under 1 % of steps in every option** (largest: 0.72 %, D16 at 3.0 m).
  More tags and a longer range raise position fixes (4 % → 37 %) and the map, not full fixes.
- **With good odometry (study, carpet) B2 is not better than dead reckoning in any option:** B0's median ATE is
  1.4–2.2 cm, B2's 1.6–3.1 cm; B2 is lower in 14–60 of 150 episodes. A position fix adds the tag's own error to an
  estimate that was already good. The real car's odometry will decide whether this holds.
- **With 2 % drift the heading is never corrected under the frozen sigmas** (final heading error 21–26° for B2 and B0
  alike), position fixes are refused once the heading is off by more than 3° (prior kept 5–32 %, no map update on
  those steps), and the map suffers (A at 2.5 m: 6.4 tags seen, 4.7 mapped; 89 of 149 duplicated pairs mapped as two).
- **Full fixes that correct the heading need both changes: measured DV sigmas and more tags.** C20 at 3.0 m, drift2:
  final heading error 10.5° against 23.0° for dead reckoning, ATE 12.2 against 14.9 cm, B2 lower in 121 of 150.
  With 12 tags the same sigmas give 20.5° against 22.2°.
- **Range limit:** 2.0 → 2.5 → 3.0 m maps 4.2 → 6.3 → 7.2 of 12 tags (A) and 8.7 → 11.6 → 13.9 of 20 (C20); all three
  duplicated pairs are seen in 150 of 150 episodes only from 2.5 m (A at 2.0 m: a pair is seen in 62 of 150 episodes).
  Table 8 (C20): 3.5 m maps 17.8 and 4.0 m 19.7 of 20, with the noise model extrapolated.
- **Near limit (0.74 m for 160 mm tags, 0.53 m for 100 mm):** it hid a tag in 3–8 % of B2's steps with 160 mm tags
  and in 0.2–2 % with 100 mm tags. In EXPLORE the effect is small: position-fix share rises by 4–6 points with the
  small tags, tags mapped do not change. What it does change: (a) a scan done within about 0.75 m of a wall sees none
  of that wall's tags, and B2 drives up to 0.35 m from an edge, so some scans are blind (start S3: 11 % of steps);
  (b) no fix is possible close to a wall; (c) `done_range_m` (0.55 m) is inside the 160 mm near limit, so a
  GO_TO_BEACON approach loses its target at 0.74 m and ends in the prompt's ASK branch, not DONE. EXPLORE does not use
  `done_range_m`; the real sessions planned are EXPLORE. With 100 mm tags (0.53 m) a DONE by sight is just possible.
- **Wrong matches: 0 in every row but one** (3 in C16 at 3.0 m, drift2). Duplicated IDs are mapped as two beacons
  whenever both copies are seen and the heading has not drifted.
- **Clearance.** The forward rule (0.35 m ahead along the heading, from the true pose) lets the footprint come within
  a few centimetres of a wall on an oblique approach; under carpet veer it touched in 1–6 of 150 episodes in several
  rows (Table 1c). With the driving area moved in by 0.4 m the closest approach is 34–35 cm and nothing else changes
  (Table 6). On the car the validator cannot see a wall that has no tag in view; a geofence from the taped start pose
  and odometry on the inset polygon is the way to get the same rule.
- **A constant +2° bearing offset** (the yaw uncertainty before the pilot) costs B2 about 0.6–0.8 cm of ATE and
  removes nothing else (Table 7).
- **"study" preset (Table 3):** with the placeholder's 60° view and no near limit, full fixes are 1.0–1.7 % and
  position fixes add its larger tag noise (study odometry: B2 4.6–6.6 cm against B0 1.5–1.7 cm). The camera preset is the one to plan with.

## Recommendations (for Xiaohui to decide)

**(a) Range limit: 3.0 m, if the pilot's far part confirms it; 2.5 m otherwise; not 2.0 m.** At 2.0 m B2 maps 4.2 of
12 tags and sees a duplicated pair in 62 of 150 episodes; 2.5 m gives 6.3 and every pair in every episode;
3.0 m gives 7.2 (13.9 of 20 with the larger layout) at the same episode length. The simulated detector's range error
at 3 m (about 3 cm) is a tenth of the 0.3 m association gate. Costs: the prompt's "2.0" has to change with it for
the LLM arms on real-room runs, and under drift a longer range brings more "B2 stopped" endings (C20: 6 → 10 of 150
from 2.0 to 3.0 m with frozen sigmas).

**(b) Layout: C20, i.e. keep the 12 printed pages where the mounting sheet puts them and add 8 pages of the same
160 mm size.** Reprint nothing; print 8 new pages (tag36h11, 160 mm black, same sheet format, each with an ID used
once): ID 1 on the angled section 20 cm from corner 4; ID 7, 5, 4, 6, 11 on the window wall 14, 125, 215, 314 and
404 cm from corner 3; ID 8 and 9 on the 295 cm wall 200 and 269 cm from corner 5 (world file
`real-room-plus8-160mm-nominal.json`; these go on the mounting sheet with a slide range like the others). Why not
the 100 mm tags (B, D16, D20): they are better on every fix number (D20 at 3.0 m, drift2, measured sigmas: 9.0 % full
fixes against 5.8 %, ATE 11.5 against 12.2 cm) and bring the near limit to 0.53 m, but all 20 pages would be
reprinted and re-cut, and 3.0 m is at their simulated far limit (40 px at 3.1 m) with no margin; at 160 mm the
margin is large (40 px at 4.8 m). If GO_TO_BEACON runs on the car are wanted, the 100 mm tags are the option that
makes DONE by sight possible. C16 (4 more pages: the first four of the list: angled 20, window 215, 125, 314) is the
smaller step: 6.8 % full fixes at 3.0 m with measured sigmas against 8.2 % for C20.

**(c) The driving area: move it in by 0.4 m on every side** (tape, or a geofence in the car backend). Closest
footprint approach 34–35 cm instead of contact in some carpet episodes; tags mapped and episode length unchanged.

**(d) Not a layout question but the largest lever: a real-run DV file with the pilot's measured sigma values**
(`sigma_range_a_m`, `sigma_range_b_per_m`, `sigma_bearing_deg`). Without it, no layout gives two-tag fixes (under 1 %
of steps) and B2 cannot correct its heading; with it and C20, B2 halves the final heading error under 2 % drift.
The frozen `configs/dv.json` stays as it is for the simulated study.

**(e) The B2 driver for the car is `kinsim_room.make_b2_policy()`** (the frozen procedure plus the dead-end branch),
and it must treat an unimplemented branch as the end of the episode.

Expectation to compare the real runs with (C20, 3.0 m, measured sigmas, S1–S5): about 45–60 steps to DONE (S3 longer),
a tag in view in about 64 % of steps and two in about 30 %, full fixes in about 8 % and position fixes in about 22 %
of steps, 13–14 of 20 tags mapped, no wrong matches. With the printed layout at 2.0 m and frozen sigmas: about 50
steps, a tag in view in 25 % of steps, position fixes in 4 %, no full fix, 4 of 12 tags mapped.

true pose; the measured-sigma rows change three design values that Xiaohui has not approved for real runs.
