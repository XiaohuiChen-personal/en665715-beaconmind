# Mounting sheet — BeaconMind room layout `real-room-2`

Each tag's page goes with its **bottom edge resting on the top of the baseboard** (tag centre 23 cm above the carpet), its printed arrow up. The distance is from the named corner, along the wall at baseboard height, to the **centre of the tag**. If the spot covers an outlet or trim, slide the page along the wall within the range given and write down where its centre ended up.

Rows marked **new** are the pages added by this layout; the others are the pages already printed. The table is in wall order (each wall from its named corner), so neighbours are next to each other. A slide range is narrower than before where a new page is a neighbour: inside the ranges given, no two pages can touch.
