## Narration

`presentation/demo-voiceover.md`, generated by `python3 course-project/tools/demo_video.py narration` from
`presentation/demo-voiceover.template.md`: 13 blocks, each with its start and end time in the silent video and its
word budget at 130 words a minute; none is over budget. Blocks for the car part (`car.part`, `car.episode`) are in
the template and appear once the car part exists. The narration says only what the screen shows or the logs hold
(the model's hidden reasoning is not logged), and the video works without it: the cards and labels carry the story.
